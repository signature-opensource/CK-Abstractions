The declarations of the CKomposable configuration: what is configurable at the four levels (AppContext aspects, App
aspects, party features, client features), their options, their configuration models and the configuration attributes.

Main types: `IConfigurableTypeAttribute` and `TypeActivation` (the configurable types), `IConfigurableOption<TOwner>` and
`IConfigurableOptionAttribute` (their options), `[Configuration<TModel>]`, `[ConfigurationModel]` and `IConfigurationModel`
(the models), the `[Configuration...]` attributes, and the declarations of each level: `[AppContextAspect]`,
`[AppAspect]`, `[PartyFeature]`, `[PartyClientFeature]` with their option attributes and owner kinds.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Abstractions/blob/stable/CK.AppBuilder.Abstractions/README.md).
